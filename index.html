About Me

Multimedia Computing Student & Frontend Developer

Hi! I'm currently studying Multimedia Computing at Universiti Malaysia Sarawak (UNIMAS). I graduated with a Diploma in Information Technology (Digital Technology) from Politeknik Kuching Sarawak.

After completing my diploma, I joined Infollective Solution Company in Kuching as an intern and continued working as a Frontend Developer for over a year.

During my working experience, I learned how to develop web applications, collaborate with team members, and integrate frontend interfaces with backend systems.

I have experience using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Java, and PHP. I have also worked with MySQL, XAMPP, Yii Framework, React.js, and Android Studio.

Currently, I am continuing my studies to improve my programming skills and explore new technologies.
